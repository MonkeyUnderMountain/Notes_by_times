This is a note for reading the series of works by Zhuchao Ji and Junyi Xie about the family of rational maps on $\bbP^1_\bbC$.
The following are some main theorems and conjectures that I want to understand.

\begin{mainthm}
	The (course) moduli space of rational maps of degree $d$ on $\bbP^1_\bbC$ is an affine variety of dimension $2d-2$.
\end{mainthm}


\begin{mainthm}
	The DAO holds for $\dim S = 1$.\Yang{}
\end{mainthm}

\begin{mainthm}
	The multiplier spectrum map $\Lambda: \calM_d(\bbC) \to \bbC^{2d-2}/\frakS_{2d-2}$ is quasi-finite.\Yang{}
\end{mainthm}

\begin{mainthm}
	The multiplier spectrum map is generically injective.
\end{mainthm}

\begin{mainthm}
	The UBC holds for dimension $1$ family. \Yang{}
\end{mainthm}
