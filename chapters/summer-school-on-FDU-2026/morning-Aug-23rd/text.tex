\section{morning-Aug-23rd: Abelian varieties of Weil type}

Let $X$ be a smooth projective variety over $\mathbb{C}$.
Recall that
\[ Z^p(X) = \text{the group of codimension $p$ algebraic cycles on $X$} \]
And we have the cycle class map
\[ Z^p(X) \to H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Z}) \]
And Let $H_{dg}^p(X) = H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Z}) \cap H^{p,p}(X)$ be the group of Hodge classes of degree $2p$.
The class map factors as
\[ Z^p(X) \\xrightarrow{\psi} H_{dg}^p(X) \hookrightarrow H^{2p}(X, \bbQ) \]

\begin{conjecture}[Hodge conjecture]
	The map $Z^p(X)_\bbQ \to H_{dg}^p(X)_\bbQ$ is surjective.
\end{conjecture}

For $p = 0,n$, this is trivial.
For $p=1$, it is given by Lefschetz hyperplane theorem, and $p = n -1$ is by Hard Lefschetz theorem.
In general it is wildly open.

This week, we will discuss that the Hodge conjecture hold for abelian sixfold of Weil type of disc 1.
And this implies Hodge conjecture holds for every abelian fourfold.


\subsection{}

Let $D^p(X) \subset H_{dg}^p(X)$ be the subalg generated by $H_{dg}^0$ and $H_{dg}^2$.
Then we have $D^p(X) \subset \Im \psi$.
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Mattack: $X$ be a general abelian variety, then $D^p(X) = H_{dg}^p(X)$.
	\item Tate: if $X$ is product of elliptic curves, then $D^p(X) = H_{dg}^p(X)$.
	\item Mumford: there exists a simple abelian variety $X$ of dimension $4$ such that $D^2(X) \subsetneqq H_{dg}^2(X)$.
	\item Tankev: if $X$ is simple and $\dim X$ is prime, then $D^p(X) = H_{dg}^p(X)$.
	\item Mooren-Zarihin: Let $X$ be a simple abelian fourfold. If $D^p(X) \subsetneqq H^p_{dg}(X)$, then $X$ is of ``Weil type''.
	\item Abelian fourfold has following classification:
	      \begin{itemize}
		      \item simple:
		      \item $2+2$:
		      \item $3+1$:
	      \end{itemize}
	\item Hodge conjecture holds for Weil type abelian variety of fixed disc of dim $2n$ implies holds for Weil o \Yang{}
\end{enumerate}
